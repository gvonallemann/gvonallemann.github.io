Sand Surfers – Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026

The short version: your game progress stays on your phone. We don't ask for your real name, email, contacts or location. Ads and purchases are handled by Google. If you join the world Top 20, your nickname, flag and score are public, and you can remove them at any time.

Who we are

Sand Surfers ("the game") is made by Optymara ("we", "us"). Questions about privacy can be sent to admin@supplychainhub.ai.

Information stored on your device

Your coins, riders, upgrades, missions, best scores and settings are saved on your phone only. We don't receive this information. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

World Top 20 leaderboard

If one of your runs makes the world Top 20, you can choose to add a nickname and a country flag. When you do, or when you change them, the game sends:

DataWhyVisible to other players?
Nickname (3–12 letters you choose)Shown on the Top 20Yes
Country (you pick it; the game suggests one from your phone's language)Shown as a flagYes
Score, distance and run lengthRanking, and checking that scores are possibleScore only
A random ID created by the gameSo only your phone can update your entry, and to handle reportsNo

Please don't use your real name. Nicknames are checked for rude words, and players can report names. Reported names may be hidden or removed, and devices that break the rules may be blocked from the leaderboard.

The leaderboard is stored with Supabase, which also keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security. Entries are kept until you remove them or the leaderboard is reset.

Removing your entry: open the game, tap Top 20, then Remove me. Your nickname, flag and score are deleted straight away. You can also email us to ask.

Advertising

The game shows ads using Google AdMob, and may use other ad networks through AdMob (such as Unity Ads, Mintegral and Meta Audience Network). These services may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. They use it to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. To confirm purchases and restore them, the game uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for this game.

Children

The game is designed for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has shared personal information through the leaderboard, contact us and we will delete it.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers listed above, to run the game, and when the law requires it.

Security

Data sent by the game travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Leaderboard records can only be changed through checked server functions.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or POPIA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to how it's used. Contact us at admin@supplychainhub.ai and we'll respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
