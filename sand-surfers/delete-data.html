Sand Surfers by Optymara

Remove your name and score from the world Top 20

You can do this yourself in the game at any time:

  1. Open Sand Surfers.
  2. On the home screen, tap Top 20.
  3. Tap Remove me, then confirm.

Your nickname, country flag and score are deleted from our leaderboard straight away, together with any name reports you made.

Or ask us by email

Email admin@supplychainhub.ai with the subject "Sand Surfers data deletion" and the nickname you used on the Top 20. We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.
